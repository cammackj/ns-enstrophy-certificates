## Interpretation

The declared concrete gate package is closed at certificate-form level:
`Z_atlas(g34)=Z_atlas(g45)=Z_atlas(g56)=Z_atlas(g67)=empty` in the row language
of the manuscript.  This does not by itself prove the all-later
recurrence theorem.  For the K45-generated route, the
no-new-signature/X_rank gate and endpoint-margin gate are discharged
in the manuscript; after local rows and the branch envelope close,
the terminal dichotomy is a finite uncovered charged exact-extremizer
witness.  The charged-coordinate fallback sharpens failure to a
finite private exact-extremizer witness satisfying Dq_gamma[F]=0;
finite first-active charged refinement discharges that stationary
equation by row priority and the finite charged-label alphabet.
Thus the charged row is closed on the K45-generated finite-ledger
route, and the generated row rules discharge the local rows.
Branch-normality is now instantiated on that route: residual
true branches are seed/dense, finite low-min-shell fallback, or
concrete high-shell branches covered by the non-D / concrete
D-parallelogram envelope.  The actual-atlas theorem now packages
the K45 extraction alternative into the row-complete atlas,
bounded-record-gap convention, finite charged ledger, and Paper 1
persistent-defect handoff.  The K45 extraction obstruction
alternative now reduces failure of extraction to a finite ledger:
new-signature cells outside the K45-stable alphabet.  Missing
generated charged-coordinate labels are supplied by generated
charged-ledger completion and are not a separate live gate.  The
new-signature ledger is discharged by finite row-priority rank
descent in the manuscript.  Thus the K45-generated structural
route is closed at manuscript-proof level.  The audit still marks
`unconditional_all_later_closed` false unless an explicit
all-later manifest is supplied, because that field records the
artifact-manifest condition rather than the manuscript theorem.
